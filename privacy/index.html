Website Privacy Notice

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This notice covers the website of Anima Business Consulting (Pty) Ltd ("Anima", "we"). Our apps have their own policies, for example the Block Pop privacy policy.

What this website collects

Nothing directly. The site has no accounts, no contact forms, no cookies and no analytics or advertising scripts.

Like any website, our hosting provider may keep ordinary technical logs, such as the address of the device that visited and the time. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.

If you email or phone us

We use your name, contact details and message only to reply and to carry out any work you ask us to do. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. We keep correspondence for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was given, or as the law requires.

Your rights

We handle personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). You can ask us what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to the address below.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
