# Anima: logo brief

## About
Anima Business Consulting (Pty) Ltd is a South African consulting company
(Johannesburg). It advises financial services firms (investment accounting,
systems, process re-engineering, training) and also builds software and
publishes apps. Clients include large listed financial institutions.

## What we need
A logo for the website, App Store developer pages and documents.

1. Primary logo: symbol plus the word "Anima", with "Business Consulting" as an optional smaller line.
2. Symbol on its own (square), used as the favicon and app-store profile image. It must still read at 16 x 16 px.
3. One-colour versions: all dark, and all white (for dark backgrounds).

## Feel
Calm and professional. Trustworthy, clear, modern. Not flashy, no gradients needed.
It should suit a bank's procurement team and still sit comfortably next to
friendly consumer apps (a children's game and a family budgeting app).

## Current placeholder (please replace, see brand/current/)

- Navy  #14233b
- Teal  #1f6f78 (accent), #7fc4cb (light teal)

## Deliverables
- SVG (vector) for every version, and PNG exports with transparent background
- Symbol as a 1024 x 1024 px PNG
- Colour codes (hex) and the font name(s) used, with font licence if not free

## Where it appears
- Website header and footer (small, about 34 px tall in the header)
- Browser tab icon
- Letterhead and email signature (later)
