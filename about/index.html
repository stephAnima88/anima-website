About us

A consulting company for investment operations.

Anima Business Consulting is a South African company that helps financial services firms improve how their investment operations run.

What we focus on

Investment accounting frameworks, systems, processes and training.

We work with the people who run investment operations day to day, and with the technology teams who support them.

Most of our work sits where those meet: a process that depends on a system, a system that needs to change, a team that needs to be ready for it. We also build custom software when the right tool does not yet exist.
