Block Pop

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Block Pop is a game for young children, published by Anima Business Consulting (Pty) Ltd, a company registered in South Africa. It is built so that it does not need any information about you or your child.

What we collect

Nothing. Block Pop does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information, from adults or from children. There are no accounts, no sign-ups and no analytics. There is no advertising and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

What stays on the device

The game remembers a few simple things on the device it is played on, so it can pick up where you left off: which pictures have been finished, whether the sound is on or off, whether day or night colours are chosen, and whether the unlock purchase has been made. This stays on the device. We never see it and it is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Internet

The game does not connect to the internet by itself. The only exception is the optional one-time purchase that unlocks every picture. That purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details or your Apple account. We only learn from Apple, on your device, whether the purchase has been made.

Children

Block Pop is designed for children and follows the rules for apps in the App Store's Kids Category. It has no ads, and it has no links that lead out of the app. The purchase screen is behind a simple grown-up check, and Apple's Ask to Buy applies if you use Family Sharing.

Changes

If this ever changes, the new policy will be posted here with a new date.
