Block Pop

Support

Block Pop is a block-popping game for young children. Need a hand? Here are the common questions.

How do I unlock all the pictures?

On the first screen, tap "Unlock all shapes". It is meant for grown-ups, so you will be asked to solve a small sum first. A one-time purchase then unlocks every picture, including the ones we add later.

I bought it before, or I have a new device

Open "Unlock all shapes", solve the sum, and tap "Restore purchase". Use the same Apple account you bought with.

How do I go back to the start screen while playing?

Press and hold the house button in the top corner for about a second. A quick tap does nothing, on purpose, so little fingers do not leave the game by accident.

Is my child's information collected?

No. See the privacy policy.

Something is wrong

Please write to info@animabusinessconsulting.net and tell us which device and iOS version you use, and what happened.
