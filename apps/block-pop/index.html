App

Block Pop

A calm, colourful game for little hands.

Each picture is a glowing 3D shape made of tiny blocks. Tap a shooter and it pops the blocks that match its colour. Drag a finger to turn the picture around and find more blocks to pop. When the last block pops, there is a star, a happy little tune, and a new picture waiting.

Made for small fingers

  • Big buttons and bright pictures. No reading needed.
  • There is no losing, no game over and no timers.
  • Gentle sounds, with a sound button for quiet time.
  • Day and night colours, so it looks good at bedtime too.
  • No ads. No accounts. No data collected. It does not need the internet.

Pictures

Free pictures include animal friends, farm, jungle, ocean, dinosaurs, treats, cars and boats. A one-time purchase unlocks every picture, now and later, with no subscription. The purchase is behind a simple grown-up check, and Apple's Ask to Buy works with Family Sharing.

Block Pop works on iPhone and iPad.
